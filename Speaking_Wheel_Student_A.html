Useful C1 language

Buying time

  • That's a thought-provoking question…
  • It's not something I've given much thought to, but…
  • Let me think about that for a moment.

Giving and developing opinions

  • As far as I'm concerned, …
  • I'd go as far as to say that…
  • What's more, … / On top of that, …
  • A case in point would be…

Balancing and conceding

  • Admittedly, … but on the other hand…
  • That said, … / Having said that, …
  • It largely depends on…
  • While I see the point, I'm not entirely convinced that…

Concluding

  • All things considered, …
  • On balance, I'd say…
